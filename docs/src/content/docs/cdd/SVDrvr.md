## Purpose and responsibility

Complex driver for the power stage (gate drive and pulse-width modulation, including the non-AUTOSAR pulse-width-modulation driver and motor-control output conversion).

## Usage notes

The `SVDrvr` component is scheduled through the Runtime Environment: periodic runnables run in their assigned operating-system task/rate, initialisation runnables run during startup, and client-server (`Scom`) operations are invoked by testers, manufacturing services or other components. Calibration constants are tuned via the measurement and calibration tool chain (see [Tools and Build System](../tools/)).
